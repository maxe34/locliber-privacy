LocLiber — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 14 October 2026

Română · Русский · English

LocLiber is an iPhone and Android app that tells driving-licence candidates in Moldova when an earlier exam slot opens at their chosen centre, instead of waiting out the usual five to six weeks. The data controller is Maxim Cucerenco, Republic of Moldova, reachable at cucerenco.m23@gmail.com.

The app only works if you entrust it with the details from your identity card. This policy therefore states exactly what is collected, who sees it, and how you delete it.

What we collect

  • Identity documents: your IDNP (national identity number), first and last name, the series, number and issue date of your identity card, and optionally your medical certificate number.
  • What you are waiting for: the centre, the exam type (ordinary or urgent), the licence category (for example B, manual), the date you need a slot before, and — if you tell us — the date of a failed attempt.
  • For notifications: the push identifier Apple (on iPhone) or Google through Firebase Cloud Messaging (on Android) issues for your phone, and a randomly generated account identifier.
  • For the subscription: the transaction Apple signs or the purchase token Google Play issues, its identifier, and the date the subscription expires.
  • Your consent: the version of the text you accepted and when you accepted or withdrew it.
  • The language you chose in the app.

Why we collect it

The basis is your consent, given in the app before anything is entered, under Law no. 195/2024 on personal data protection, in force since 23 August 2026. (The consent text in the app still cites Law no. 133/2011, which the new law replaced.) The identity details are necessary because eservicii.gov.md shows availability only to an identified person. Without them there is no product.

How your details are used in queries

The app on your phone queries nothing. A service on our server checks your centre's calendar periodically, identifying itself with the details of a person waiting at that centre.

Sometimes that person is you. ASP limits how many queries one IDNP may make, so for each centre the queries are made in turn, under one subscriber's identity, and the calendar that comes back serves everyone waiting at that centre. The app shows you when it is your turn, because the daily limit applies to your IDNP. What comes back is the centre's public availability, not other people's data.

We never book on your behalf and never change anything in your account on the portal. Your documents are never shown to another user of the app.

Who else sees it

  • ASP / eservicii.gov.md — receives the queries made in your name, exactly as it would if you checked yourself.
  • Apple (on iPhone) and Google (on Android, through Firebase Cloud Messaging and Google Play) — deliver notifications to your phone and process the subscription payment. Card details belong to them; we never see them.

Nobody else. The app carries no advertising, no analytics. The only third-party components are the platform's notification and payment services (Firebase Cloud Messaging and Google Play Billing, on Android), used for those two purposes only. Your data is never sold, rented, published, or used for any purpose other than the one above.

Where it is kept, and how it is protected

On our own server, in a Postgres database. The identity fields — IDNP, first and last name, identity-card number and medical certificate number — are encrypted (AES-GCM) before they reach the database, so a copy of the database is not enough to read them. Traffic between the app and the server is encrypted (HTTPS).

The database is copied every night. The copies are kept for 14 days on the same server, and the most recent one also on the operator's computer, whose disk is encrypted, in the Republic of Moldova. In the copies too, the identity fields stay encrypted.

The server never hands the documents back to the app: when you view your profile, the IDNP appears masked. The account identifier lives in the phone's Keychain, not in files that end up in a backup.

How long we keep it

We keep your identity documents for as long as you use them: while you have a live watch, and for 6 months after your last watch ends (or after you entered them, if you never added a watch). Then they are erased automatically; if you come back, the app asks for them again.

You can delete the account from the app at any time: Your details → Delete account. The identity documents and every watch are erased from the system immediately, and notifications stop. They disappear from the backups within 14 days.

One record remains: that you gave consent and later withdrew it, with the dates. The law requires us to be able to show that processing had a basis, and this record contains none of your documents.

Your rights

Under Law no. 195/2024 you have the right to access your data, to correct it, to erase it, to restrict its processing, to data portability, to object, and to withdraw consent at any time — withdrawal takes two taps in the app and needs no message to us. For anything else, write to cucerenco.m23@gmail.com; we answer within one month. You may also complain to the National Centre for Personal Data Protection of the Republic of Moldova.

Children

The app is not directed at people under 16 and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

Changes

Any change is published on this page with a new effective date. If a change alters what we collect or why, the app asks for your consent again.
